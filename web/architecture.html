Evidence stays with its source

Private documents and employer listings inform explicit requirements. Source conflicts are surfaced for review. Browsing is performed by your research agent; the local app does not autonomously crawl job boards.

THE MATHEMATICS YOU CAN CHANGE

What does y = wx + b mean here?

Each x is an evidence assessment from 0 to 1. Each w is the importance of that requirement. The intercept b is zero. This version uses a transparent rubric, not trained neural weights.

Unknown evidence contributes zero to the conservative score. Its possible contribution is shown separately in the API. A mandatory eligibility gap blocks a recommendation regardless of the score.

A learning loop with boundaries

Private revision history → optional category-only feedback → aggregated evaluation → human-reviewed version → regression tests → release. No automatic retraining, no hidden upload of CVs, and no promise of zero error.

University ↔ Industry ↔ Government forms the Triple Helix. Individuals retain decision rights across those relationships. The 17 SDGs supply impact themes; the five IDG dimensions support reflection. PSR and PRR are experimental, voluntary reflection concepts, excluded from hiring scores.

Python runs matching and SQLite persistence. Browser HTML/CSS/JavaScript handles review and this 3D diagram. JSON APIs and MCP expose the same scoring function. A separate future billing boundary verifies payment before metered service access.
