# luna.theory, PSR, PRR and development goals

The connection is a design intention, not measured impact or official UN/IDG endorsement. A matching service can help someone recognise options, identify a learning gap and decide where to contribute. It cannot guarantee fulfilment.

These lenses label opportunities chosen by the user, not a candidate's moral worth. A tag is not evidence of impact. Core intended connections are SDGs 4, 8, 9, 10 and 17; establish outcome indicators before claiming contributions.

## Five IDG dimensions

**Being:** clarify values and constraints. **Thinking:** examine evidence and uncertainty. **Relating:** consider who is affected. **Collaborating:** identify partners and ask for help. **Acting:** choose a next step, reflect and revise. Use the five dimension labels; the number of subskills varies across framework editions and is not hard-coded.

## Experimental PSR and PRR

PSR means Personal Social Responsibility in luna.theory. A future optional self-reflection could record completion of user-chosen commitments, with consent and evidence; no universal numerical PSR scale is validated here.

PRR means Personal Recommendation Rate in this project. A proposed private usefulness measure is `recommendations the person found useful / recommendations they reviewed`, always shown with the sample size and time window. It is a satisfaction signal subject to selection bias, not accuracy, employability or trustworthiness. Neither PSR nor PRR is included in employment scoring or exported to employers.

## Triple Helix

The established Triple Helix concerns **university, industry and government**. Research and learning can inform services; organisations provide implementation contexts; public institutions shape enabling conditions. Citizens retain agency across the system. Calling citizens a fourth institutional strand describes a Quadruple Helix extension, not the original three-part model.
